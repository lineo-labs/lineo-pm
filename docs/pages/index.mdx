# Welcome to Lineo-PM

**Lineo-PM** is an open-source, decision-driven planning engine built for teams that need to understand *what happens next* — not just track what's happening now.

> Move an activity. Watch the dependencies cascade. Know instantly what changed.

Lineo-PM models your project schedule as a dependency graph. Every change you make — dragging a task, adjusting a duration, switching a scenario — propagates through the entire plan in real time, so you always see the downstream impact before you commit to a decision.

## Who's It For?
Lineo-PM is designed for project managers, product leaders, and executives who are accountable for delivery and need a tool that supports decision-making in the face of uncertainty. If you've ever updated a project plan only to realize it was already wrong by the time you finished —
