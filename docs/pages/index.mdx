# Welcome to Lineo-PM

**Lineo-PM** is an open-source, decision-driven planning engine built for teams that need to understand *what happens next* — not just track what's happening now.

> Move an activity. Watch the dependencies cascade. Know instantly what changed.

## What is Lineo-PM?

Lineo-PM is not a traditional task manager. It is a **planning engine** that models your project schedule as a dependency graph. Every change you make — dragging a task, adjusting a duration, switching a scenario — propagates through the entire plan in real time, so you always see the downstream impact before you commit to a decision.

It combines three core capabilities:

- **Dependency propagation** — cascading schedule updates across linked tasks
- **Scenario planning** — persistent alternative timelines you can compare against your baseline
- **Monte Carlo simulation** — probabilistic risk analysis that tells you how likely your project is to slip, and by how much

## Key Features

- Interactive Gantt chart with drag-and-drop rescheduling
- Instant cascading propagation when tasks move
- Scenario engine: create, compare, and promote alternative plans
- Monte Carlo simulation with per-task risk levels
- Dependency lock highlighting to visualize critical chains
- Narrative updates for decision audit trails
- Time-first UX designed for executives and project leaders

## Who It's For

Lineo-PM is designed for **project managers, founders, CTOs, product leaders, and executives** — anyone who makes decisions that affect project timelines and needs to understand the consequences before they act.
